FAQ

Common questions

Is Belongix really free?
Yes — the Starter plan is completely free forever with no credit card required. It includes Job Board access, Salary Intelligence, Bexi AI (10 queries/day), 5 courses, and Career Score. Pro (₹499/month) and Premium (₹999/month) unlock unlimited features.
How is Belongix different from Naukri or LinkedIn?
Belongix combines what you'd normally get from 5 different apps — jobs, salary data, AI guidance, courses, and 1-on-1 mentors — in one platform. Our biggest differentiator is Bexi AI, a 24/7 career guide trained specifically on India's job market. No other Indian platform has this.
Is my data safe on Belongix?
Yes. Your data is stored securely using Supabase with encryption. We never sell your data to advertisers or share it with employers without your explicit permission. You can delete your account at any time from dashboard Settings.
How do I become a mentor on Belongix?
Visit the Mentors page and fill out the "Become a Mentor" application form. We look for professionals with 6+ years of experience. Our team reviews all applications within 48 hours and your profile goes live after approval.
Do you offer corporate or college plans?
Yes! We offer custom upskilling packages for IT teams and placement preparation packages for colleges. Email us at teambelongix@gmail.com with "Corporate Plans" or "College Partnership" in the subject line.
How do I cancel my Pro or Premium subscription?
You can cancel anytime from Dashboard → Settings → Plan Management. You'll keep Pro/Premium access until the end of your current billing period. No hidden fees or penalties.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Use the contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the subject. Please include your browser, device type, and steps to reproduce. We prioritise bug fixes and aim to resolve critical issues within 24 hours.
