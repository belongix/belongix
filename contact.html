Frequently asked questions
Are the courses really free?
Yes — all 268 courses are free to browse and start. Certificates are available on completion. Check individual course pages for details.
Is the Resume Builder free?
Yes, the ATS Resume Builder is completely free — including the ATS Readiness Score, keyword analysis, AI suggestions, and PDF export.
How do I access my certificate?
Certificates are issued on course completion and accessible from your Dashboard. You can download and share them directly.
I can't log into my account. What do I do?
Try resetting your password from the sign-in screen. If that doesn't work, email us at hello@belongix.in and we'll help.
Does the AI Resume Builder invent facts?
No. Our AI only works with what you enter. It never invents companies, dates, or metrics. Any AI suggestion is clearly labelled and always editable by you.
How do I suggest a new course or topic?
Send us a message above or email hello@belongix.in with your suggestion. We actively add courses based on what learners need.
