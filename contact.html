Common Questions
Before you write — check if we've already answered it
Are all the courses really free? ▾
Browsing and previewing courses is free — no account needed. To enroll in a course, complete lessons, and earn a Belongix certificate, you need a Pro plan at ₹499/month. Pro also includes Career Score, exclusive learning paths, priority support, and early access to new courses.
How do I earn a Belongix certificate? ▾
Upgrade to Pro (₹499/month), enroll in any course, complete all lessons, and your certificate unlocks automatically at 100%. You can download it as a PDF and share it directly to LinkedIn. Certificates are tied to your Belongix account — sign up at belongix.in to get started.
I found a bug or something isn't working. What do I do? ▾
Use the form above and select "Bug report" — please include your browser, what page it happened on, and what you expected vs. what actually happened. Screenshots help a lot. We take bugs seriously and fix them fast.
Can I suggest a course or domain that's missing? ▾
Yes, and we genuinely want to hear it. Use the "Feature idea" topic on the form above. We already have 1019 courses across 22 domains, but we're constantly expanding. Good suggestions often make it into the platform within weeks.
I'm interested in a partnership or corporate training. Who do I contact? ▾
Use the form above and select "Partnership". Tell us about your organisation, what you're looking for (corporate training, college integration, content collaboration), and we'll get back to you within 2 business days. Email works too: teambelongix@gmail.com.
I forgot my password / can't log in. Help? ▾
Go to belongix.in, click "Sign In", then use "Continue with Google" if you signed up with Google. For email accounts, a password reset option is available in Dashboard → Settings → Change Password. If you're still stuck, use the form above with the topic "Account issue".
Do you have a press kit or media resources? ▾
Yes — logos, screenshots, brand guidelines, and platform stats are available on request. Use the form above and select "Press / Media", or email us directly at teambelongix@gmail.com. We're happy to speak with journalists, podcasters, and content creators covering India's EdTech and startup ecosystem.
