Questions

Before you start.

Does the AI make things up?

No. Belongix AI improves the wording of what you've already written — summaries, bullet points — it doesn't invent employers, dates, metrics, or skills you haven't entered.

Will two-column templates hurt my ATS score?

Sometimes, depending on the applicant tracking system. That's why the Resume Score panel flags it directly when you pick a two-column layout, so it's a visible tradeoff, not a hidden one.

Can I keep multiple versions of my resume?

Yes — create, rename, duplicate, and switch between resume versions from the builder, e.g. one tailored per role.

What does export look like?

A print-ready, true-to-page document via your browser's print dialog — save as PDF or print directly, with the app chrome hidden automatically.
