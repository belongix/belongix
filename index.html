Frequently asked questions
Does Belongix guarantee I'll get hired?
No. Belongix is a career assistance platform — it helps you build a stronger resume, understand job fit, and prepare for interviews. It cannot guarantee interviews, offers, or employment outcomes.
Does the AI ever invent things about my experience?
No — every AI feature is explicitly instructed to work only from information you've actually provided. If a metric or detail is missing, the AI asks for it rather than making one up.
Is my data private?
Yes. Every table in Belongix's database uses row-level security — you can only ever access your own data, and administrators cannot browse user content without a specific reason. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, from Settings. Deletion is permanent and removes your career profile, resumes, applications, and AI history.
