Questions, answered

Everything you need to know.

What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System is software employers use to organize and screen applications. Belongix helps keep your resume structured and readable by common parsing systems.

Can Belongix guarantee an interview?

No. A good resume can improve clarity and relevance, but hiring decisions depend on employers, role requirements and many other factors.

Will AI invent achievements?

It should not. Belongix's intended AI workflow is evidence-first: suggestions should remain grounded in information you provide and should be reviewed before applying.

Can I create different versions?

Yes. The product is designed around maintaining tailored resume versions so you can adapt your application without losing your master content.
