How ATS actually works — and why most resumes fail
8 min

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software that parse your resume before any human sees it. 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before reaching a recruiter.

What ATS actually checks

  • Keyword match: Does your resume contain the exact words from the job description?
  • Parsability: Can the ATS extract your name, email, experience, education correctly?
  • File format: PDF (preferred) or DOCX. Not images, not Canva exports

What kills your ATS score instantly

  • Tables, columns, text boxes (ATS can't parse these)
  • Headers and footers (often ignored by parsers)
  • Images and graphics
  • Fancy fonts that don't render correctly
  • Generic filenames like "Resume.pdf" (use "ArjunKumar-SWE-Resume.pdf")

The perfect resume format for Indian tech jobs
10 min

Structure (in this order)

  1. Name + Contact (email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, city)
  2. Professional Summary (40–60 words)
  3. Work Experience (reverse chronological)
  4. Skills (exact keywords)
  5. Education
  6. Certifications (if relevant)
  7. Projects (if fresher or relevant)

Length rules

  • 0–5 years: 1 page
  • 6–12 years: 1–2 pages
  • 12+ years: 2 pages max

Font and design

Font: Arial, Calibri, or DM Sans at 10–11pt. Margins: 0.7–1 inch. White space: generous — recruiters scan in 6 seconds. Colour: max one accent colour, sparingly.

Writing achievement bullets that recruiters notice
12 min

The STAR-M formula

Every bullet: Action verb + What you did + Result (with metric)

❌ Bad: "Worked on backend systems for the payment team"
✅ Good: "Rebuilt payment processing pipeline in Go, reducing transaction latency by 40% and handling 5M daily requests with 99.99% uptime"

❌ Bad: "Led a team to improve app performance"
✅ Good: "Led 4-engineer team to optimise React bundle size by 62%, cutting Time-to-Interactive from 8.2s to 3.1s"

The numbers that impress

  • Latency/speed improvements (%)
  • Scale (DAU, requests/second, data volume)
  • Money (₹ saved, revenue generated, cost reduced)
  • Team size led
  • Time saved
  • Error rate reduction

💡 If you don't know the exact number, estimate it honestly. "~50% latency reduction" is better than "improved performance".

  • Every work bullet has an action verb + result + metric

Tailoring your resume for each job (the 20-minute method)
8 min

One generic resume for all jobs = low response rate. A tailored resume = 3× more callbacks.

The 20-minute tailoring process

  1. Copy the job description into a text doc
  2. Highlight every technical skill, tool, and methodology mentioned
  3. Check your resume — does it contain these exact words? If not, add them where true
  4. Rewrite your Summary section to mirror the JD's language
  5. Move your most relevant experience bullets to the top of each role

Example

JD says: "Experience with Kafka, distributed systems, and Go"
Your resume currently says: "Built event streaming systems with message queues"
Update to: "Built event streaming systems using Kafka with Go microservices at 50K events/second"
