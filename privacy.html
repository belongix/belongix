Plain language summary: Belongix collects only the data you give us and data needed to run the platform. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. We do not share your data with employers without your consent. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, and information generated by your use of the platform.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, improve, and personalise Belongix.

  • Provide and maintain the platform, including job board, salary insights, Bexi AI, courses, and mentor network
  • Personalise your dashboard, job recommendations, and career guidance based on your profile
  • Process mentor session bookings and Pro plan subscriptions
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, booking confirmations, password reset)
  • Send platform notifications and career tips (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Analyse platform usage to improve features and fix bugs
  • Comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations

We do not use your data to serve third-party advertisements. We do not sell your personal data.

3 Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you choose to share your profile with a mentor or employer through the platform
  • Service providers: Supabase (database & auth), Razorpay (payments), Vercel (hosting) — each bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal compliance: If required by Indian law, court order, or government authority
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, with advance notice to users
  • Aggregated data: We may share anonymised, aggregated statistics (e.g., "salary trends in Bangalore") that cannot identify individuals

🔒 Mentor network: When you book a mentor session, your name and contact details are shared with that mentor only — not with other platform users or employers.

4 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data is stored on Supabase with enterprise-grade encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — we use bcrypt hashing via Supabase Auth
  • Access to production databases is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • We conduct regular security reviews and promptly address vulnerabilities
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Razorpay — Belongix never stores card numbers or CVVs

While we take all reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by applicable regulations.

6 Your Rights

As a Belongix user, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete profile data at any time from your dashboard
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data — processed within 30 days
  • Portability: Download your profile data in JSON format from Settings → Export Data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from all marketing emails via the link in any email or from Settings
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time (this may affect platform functionality)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@belongix.in with your registered email address. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations:

  • Active accounts: Data retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Deleted accounts: Profile data deleted within 30 days; anonymised usage data may be retained for analytics
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations
  • Support communications: Retained for 2 years from the date of the last interaction
  • Mentor session records: Retained for 1 year after the session date

8 Third-Party Services

Belongix integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and file storage. Privacy Policy →
  • Razorpay — Payment processing for Pro plans and mentor sessions. Privacy Policy →
  • Google OAuth — Optional sign-in via Google. Privacy Policy →

  • Remotive / Arbeitnow APIs — Supplementary job listings. No user data is shared with these services.

9 Children's Privacy

Belongix is designed for professionals and students aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, please contact us immediately at privacy@belongix.in and we will delete the account and all associated data.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an email notification to all registered users at least 14 days before the change takes effect
  • Show an in-app notification on your dashboard

Your continued use of Belongix after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. If you do not agree, you may delete your account before the effective date.
