Privacy Policy

Last updated: this build, in active development.

What we collect

Account information (email, authentication data via Supabase Auth). Career profile information you provide directly (name, work history, education, skills, target role, and similar). Resume content you create. Job descriptions you paste for analysis. Application tracking data you enter. Records of AI feature usage, including the input and output of each AI request, for the purpose of showing you your own suggestion history and preventing abuse.

How we use it

To operate the features you use directly — building resumes, analyzing jobs, tracking applications. To provide AI-assisted suggestions, which are always shown to you for review before anything is saved or applied. We do not use your data to train AI models beyond the immediate request that generates your suggestion.

AI processing

When you use an AI feature, the relevant content (your resume text, a job description, or similar) is sent to our AI provider's API to generate a response. This happens server-side; your AI provider credentials are never exposed to your browser. AI-generated suggestions are logged so you can see your own history — they are never automatically applied to your saved data without your explicit action.

Data retention and deletion

You can export a complete copy of your stored data at any time from Settings. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data — career profile, resumes, applications, analyzed jobs, AI history — from Settings. This deletion is real and immediate; there is no "soft delete" retaining your data after you request removal, except where financial records must legally be retained (not currently applicable, since this build has no live payment processing yet).

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with the specific third-party services necessary to operate features you use: our authentication/database provider (Supabase) and our AI provider (for the specific content of each AI request you make).

Security

Your data is protected by row-level security policies ensuring you can only access your own data — this is enforced at the database level, not just in the application interface. AI provider credentials and other secrets are never present in browser-side code.

Your rights

You can access, export, and delete your data at any time through Settings, without needing to contact support first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact channel listed on our website.
