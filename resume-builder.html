FAQ
Common questions
Does a 100/100 score guarantee I'll get interviews?
+
No. A 100/100 ATS Readiness Score means your resume passes all of our measurable optimization checks. Different employers use different ATS systems with different algorithms. Our score optimizes your resume against our checks — actual employer outcomes depend on many factors including the employer's ATS configuration, the role, and the competition pool.
Will the AI invent things on my resume?
+
Never. Our AI operates on a strict accuracy-first principle. It improves the phrasing of your real experience, suggests where metrics could be added (so you can fill in real numbers), and recommends keywords that are truthful to your background. It never fabricates jobs, companies, degrees, certifications, or achievements.
Is my resume data private?
+
Yes. Your resume is private by default, stored securely with row-level security. Only you can access your data. We never share your resume with employers or third parties.
Can I use this on mobile?
+
Yes. The app is fully responsive. On mobile, you get a simplified section editor with the AI assistant at the bottom — optimized for thumb-friendly editing.
