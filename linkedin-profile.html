Why your current LinkedIn isn't getting recruiter DMs
5 min

95% of Indian professionals have LinkedIn profiles that are essentially digital resumes — static and passive. The profiles that get 10+ recruiter DMs per month are different in 5 specific ways.

What recruiters actually search for

Recruiters use LinkedIn's Boolean search to find candidates. They search for: skill keywords + city + experience level + "open to work". If your profile doesn't have these exact terms in the right places, you're invisible.

The 5 differences between a visible and invisible profile

  1. Headline: job title vs keyword-rich descriptor
  2. Skills section: 5 skills vs 15+ specific skills
  3. About section: generic summary vs specific value proposition
  4. "Open to Work": not set vs set with correct settings
  5. Activity: silent vs posting regularly

The headline formula that 5x's your profile views
8 min

The formula

[Role] | [Skills] | [Ex-Company or Achievement]

❌ Generic: "Software Engineer at Infosys"
✅ Keyword-rich: "Backend Engineer | Go · gRPC · AWS | Ex-Swiggy | Open to Product Companies"

❌ Generic: "Data Science Professional"
✅ Keyword-rich: "Data Scientist | Python · PyTorch · LLMs | Building AI products | Bangalore"

Why this works

LinkedIn search ranks profiles by keyword density in headline + skills. Adding "Go, gRPC, AWS" to your headline means you show up for all 3 searches independently.

For freshers

"Computer Science Student | Python · SQL · React | Seeking SDE roles 2026 | IIT/NIT/VIT"

💡 Add your city in the headline if you're in Bangalore, Hyderabad, or Mumbai — recruiters often filter by location. "Bangalore-based" or just "Bangalore" works.

About section, skills, and the Open to Work setting
12 min

About section (what it should contain)

3–4 sentences: what you do, what you've built, what you're looking for.
End with: "Currently open to [Role] roles at [type of company] in [cities]."
Use paragraph breaks — no one reads walls of text.

Skills section (the LinkedIn SEO lever)

Add 15+ skills. Be specific: not "Programming" but "Python", "Go", "Java". Recruiters filter by specific skills. Get endorsements from colleagues — 5+ endorsements signals credibility.

The "Open to Work" setting

Settings: Go to your profile → "Open to" → "Finding a new job" → Add roles, locations, job types. Select "Recruiters only" (not visible to your current employer).

This alone increases your DMs by 3–5×.

Posting content that makes recruiters come to you
10 min

The highest-ROI LinkedIn activity: one post every 2 weeks. Not motivational quotes — specific career insights from your actual work.

Post formats that work for tech professionals

  • Problem → Solution: "We were handling 10K orders/minute at Diwali. Our API was timing out. Here's how we fixed it in 48 hours..."
  • Lesson learned: "I failed my system design interview 3 times. Here's what I changed to pass the 4th"
  • Data point: "I sent 200 cold LinkedIn messages to recruiters. Here are the ones that got replies"
  • Resource list: "5 free resources that helped me switch from IT services to product company"

💡 The hook is everything. Your first line determines if anyone reads further. Make it specific and counterintuitive: "I got a ₹32 LPA offer after 47 rejections. Here's what changed on attempt 48."

Cold outreach that gets responses (templates included)
8 min

The connection request template

"Hi [Name], I noticed you work on [specific product/team] at [Company] — I've been following [specific thing] and found [insight]. I'd love to connect and learn more about the engineering culture there."

The follow-up DM for job opportunities

"Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. I'm currently exploring [Role] opportunities at [Company] — I have [X years] of experience in [skill] and recently [specific achievement]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call to learn more about the team?"

The referral request (the most underused strategy)

"Hi [Name], I see you're at [Company] — I've applied for the [Role] position (Req ID: #XXXX). Would you be comfortable referring me? I'd be happy to share my resume and answer any questions. No worries at all if not!"

Referrals get 10× higher interview conversion than cold applications at most Indian product companies.
