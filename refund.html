Refund Policy

Last updated: this build, in active development.

Honest current status: this build of Belongix does not yet have a live, connected payment system. There is no real subscription to purchase and therefore nothing to refund at this stage. This page exists as a placeholder for the real policy that will apply once payments are actually implemented and enabled.

What this policy will cover, once payments exist

When subscription billing is live, this page will document: the specific refund window for a first-time subscription, how mid-cycle cancellations are handled, whether partial-period refunds are offered, and the exact process for requesting one. None of that exists to describe accurately yet, so we're not going to write speculative claims here that don't match anything you can actually do on the platform today.
