Our Journey

How we got here

Building in public — here's our story so far.

💡
Early 2025
The idea sparks
Frustrated by scattered learning resources and no clear structure for Indian developers, we started asking: why doesn't India have a proper career learning platform built for us?
⚡
Mid 2025
Building starts
First lines of code. We built the Course Catalogue, Career Score, and Certificate system — the three tools we wished we had when we were starting our own careers.
🚀
Late 2025
1000+ courses live
We curated and structured 1019 courses across 22 domains — Full Stack to AI/ML, DSA to Cybersecurity. Every single course includes a verified Belongix certificate.
🏆
2026
12,000+ certificates issued
Indian professionals are completing courses, earning certificates, and growing their careers. We're just getting started — the roadmap ahead keeps us up at night.

Our Values

What we stand for

Four principles that shape every decision we make at Belongix.

🌟
Honesty
No inflated numbers. No fake stats. We'd rather tell you the truth and show you less than mislead you. Real courses, real certificates, real progress.
🇮🇳
India First
Built for Indian professionals. Courses chosen for India's job market. Career paths designed for what companies here actually hire for — not adapted from the US.
