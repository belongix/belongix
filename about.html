Our Journey

How we got here

Building in public — here's our story so far.

💡
Early 2025
The idea sparks
Frustrated by scattered career tools and outdated learning resources, we started asking: why doesn't India have a proper career learning platform built for us?
⚡
Mid 2025
Building starts
First lines of code. We built the Course Catalogue, Career Score, and Certificate system — the three tools we wished we had when we were starting out.
🚀
Late 2025
1000+ courses live
We curated and structured 1019 courses across 22 domains — Full Stack to AI/ML, DSA to Cybersecurity. Every course includes a verified Belongix certificate.
🏆
2026
12,000+ certificates issued
Indian professionals are completing courses, earning certificates, and growing their careers. We're just getting started — the roadmap ahead is exciting.

Our Values

What we stand for

Four principles that shape every decision we make.

🌟
Honesty
No inflated numbers. No fake stats. We'd rather tell you the truth and show you less than mislead you. Real courses, real certificates, real progress.
🇮🇳
India First
Built for Indian professionals. Courses chosen for India's job market. Career paths designed for what companies here actually hire for. Not adapted from the US.
🆓
Access for All
Core features are always free. 1000+ courses are free. Certificates are free. Career growth should not be limited by your background or budget.
🔒
Privacy First
Your data belongs to you. We never sell user data to advertisers. We never share it without permission. Simple, no exceptions.
