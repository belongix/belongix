Our Mission

Career growth shouldn't be a privilege

In India, the best career advice often comes from who you know — not what you know. If you didn't go to an IIT, or didn't have the right network, you were on your own.

We built Belongix to change that. One platform that gives every Indian professional — regardless of college, city, or background — the same tools that used to be available only to the privileged few.

Our Journey

How we got here

Building in public — here's our story so far.

Early 2025
The idea sparks
Frustrated by scattered career tools and outdated salary data, we started asking: why doesn't India have a proper career platform built for us?
Mid 2025
Building starts
First lines of code. We built the Job Board, Salary Intelligence, and Career Score — the three tools we wished we had when we were job hunting.
Late 2025
Bexi AI joins the team
We trained Bexi — our AI career guide — on India's job market. She answers any career question, 24/7, completely free. No competitor has this.
2026
Mentor Network launches
50+ verified professionals join as mentors from Google, Swiggy, Razorpay, and more — available for 1-on-1 sessions.
Today
Building with the community
2,400+ professionals, 1,500+ jobs, and growing every day. We're building this in public — with feedback from real users like you.
